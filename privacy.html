日本語

もぐらパニックで「共通ランキングに挑戦」を選ぶと、公開ニックネーム、命中の情報、得点、完了時刻をサーバーに送ります。ニックネームと得点・順位は他の利用者に公開されます。本名や連絡先を入力しないでください。通常の練習と他の6ゲームの記録はランキングへ送りません。

サーバーで時間・命中・重複を検証しますが、自動操作を完全に排除するものではありません。無料枠を守るため、共通ランキングは1日250ラウンド、同じ接続元で10分間10回を上限にしています。上限時も練習は遊べます。

English

Asobi Yokocho is a free browser arcade. Updated October 28, 2026. No ads, analytics, payments or accounts.

Favorites, language, plays, local scores, café and Muscle Gym progress stay in this browser. Clear site data in your browser to delete them. They do not sync between devices.

Choosing Enter shared ranking in Mole Panic sends your public nickname, hit events, score and completion time to the server. Your nickname, score and rank are public. Do not use personal information. Practice and the other six games do not upload scores.

To limit repeated submissions, the server derives a daily rotating identifier from your IP; raw IP addresses are not saved in the ranking database. Sessions expire after one hour, rate identifiers after 24 hours and rankings after 90 days. Expired data is removed gradually on later requests, so physical deletion can take longer without traffic. Weekly rankings reset Monday 00:00 UTC.

Server checks limit forged scores, duplicates and out-of-time hits, but cannot completely prevent automated play. To protect the free plan, shared rounds are limited to 250 per UTC day and 10 per IP per 10 minutes. Practice remains available.

Operated as Asobi Yokocho. The hosting provider may process technical connection information.
