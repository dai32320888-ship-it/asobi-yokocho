運営名：あそび横丁。改善の提案や不具合報告は、サイトを案内した投稿への返信をご利用ください。個人情報や保存データの公開は不要です。

Asobi Yokocho is a free browser arcade. Updated October 27, 2026.

Favorites, language, play counts, scores, your nickname and café progress are stored in this browser only. Use a nickname, without personal or contact information. Games can run when storage is unavailable, but progress may not persist.

Game records are not sent to an operator server. Rankings belong to this device and browser. There are no ads, analytics, accounts or payments. Clear this site's data in your browser to remove saved records. Records do not sync across devices.

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Operated as Asobi Yokocho. Report issues by replying to the post that introduced the site; do not publish personal information.
